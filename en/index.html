01 / The concept

Human–Human–AI is more than a person plus a machine.

In learning and work, people do not act in isolation. They learn together, ask questions, share responsibility and make decisions. AI can support, change or undermine these relationships. The quality of a system is therefore not determined by its output alone.

Our design and review principles

Human Agency First. Fairness by Design. Observable Consequences.

Human Agency First

Can people understand, choose independently, challenge a decision and remain in control?

Fairness by Design

Is the experience understandable, inclusive, transparent and free of manipulative choice architecture?

Observable Consequences

What changes in understanding, collaboration and human capability can actually be observed?

These are orientation questions for our work with AI, not a legal compliance statement.

Our working hypothesis: Responsible AI should help people do more than work faster. It should support understanding, autonomous decisions and effective action with others. This is a testable hypothesis, not an established causal finding.

04 / Research and limitations


From usability to observable human capability.


Human–Human–AI is a conceptual research approach by Goldmonkeys / HOP. It connects human-centred design, AI literacy, governance and learning research. This is an exploratory approach to understanding how technology changes people's capabilities and relationships.


1. Usability
Is the system accessible, understandable and usable?
2. Capability
What can people understand and do independently, including without AI support?
3. Consequence
What changes in real collaboration, judgement and action?
